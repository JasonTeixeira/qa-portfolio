Interaction floor
—Every interactive element is focusable; focus ring is 2px #8FA0FF, offset 2.
—Hit targets ≥ 44px on touch surfaces; chips get invisible padding, not bigger borders.
—Color never carries meaning alone — status chips always pair hue with a word.
—Widgets are keyboard-completable: tab to option, enter to select, escape to reset.
