REPAIR LOG · JUN 2026 · 9 MIN

The invoice that emailed itself twice.

JT Jason Teixeira · founder, Sage Academy share →

Tuesday, 09:14. Finance says four customers got their monthly invoice twice. Same invoice ID, same amounts, eleven minutes apart. The billing job ran once — the logs swear it. The email service shows two sends, both "accepted." Somebody in the war room says the sentence that always precedes disaster: "it can't be the retry wrapper, we added that to fix reliability."

Here's the map we should have drawn in March, when that wrapper shipped. Between the billing job and the inbox sit four systems — and one of them cannot tell a timeout from a failure.

A retry without an idempotency key isn't reliability. It's a duplicate generator with good intentions.

The fix took forty minutes: derive the send key from the invoice ID, check the send ledger before calling the email service, and make the wrapper treat "timeout" as "unknown — go ask the ledger" instead of "failed — try again." The postmortem took longer, and taught more. Both are below, verbatim, because a repair you can inspect beats advice you have to trust.
