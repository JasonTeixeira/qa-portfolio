Most asked
How does the mastery cap actually work?

Your course score is capped by your weakest passing proof — because that's how a reviewer reads your work. The cap always names the exact repair that lifts it. Repair it and the cap releases immediately; no waiting period.

How do I claim the 14-day guarantee?

Reply to any billing email with the word "guarantee" within 14 days of your first charge. Refund is processed same-day, no form, no exit interview. Your ledger export comes with it.

What happens to my streak on vacation?

Pause your membership (Settings → Billing) and the streak freezes instead of breaking. Unpaused, recall restarts gently — two prompts a day, not a wall of debt.

Can Sprout just give me the answer?

No — by design. Sprout nudges with questions and hints, never lab solutions. The "Show the fix" button exists in every lab when you want it, but taking a hint is tracked honestly (No-Hint Repair is a badge for a reason).

Are certificates really verifiable after I cancel?

Yes. The verify endpoint is public record — curl it from anywhere, logged in or not, member or not. If a proof behind a certificate later stops holding, the certificate says so. That's what makes it worth showing.

What can my manager see on a team plan?

Verdicts, artifacts, and mastery — the same things your public profile shows. Never lesson-by-lesson activity, never time-on-page. Surveillance kills learning; we don't sell it.
