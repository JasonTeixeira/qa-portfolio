The honest summary

You get all courses while subscribed. Your artifacts are yours, forever. Don't share your account, don't scrape the content, don't resell it. We can update the service; we can't retroactively take your ledger.

§ 1
The service

Sage Academy provides courses, labs, evaluation gates, recall systems, and related tooling on a subscription basis. Features may evolve; material removals of paid functionality entitle you to a prorated refund.

§ 2
Your work is yours

Artifacts you create — memos, maps, code, postmortems — belong to you. We store and display them at your direction (e.g., your public profile) and never train models on them without explicit opt-in.

§ 3
Accounts

One person per account. Team seats are per-person and transferable by an admin. We may suspend accounts for abuse (scraping, credential sharing, harassment in community spaces) — with notice and an export.

§ 4
Verification records

Certificates and their verify endpoints are public records by design. Revocation occurs only when an underlying proof demonstrably fails — never as a commercial lever.

§ 5
Liability

The service is provided as-is; our aggregate liability is capped at the fees you paid in the trailing 12 months. Nothing here limits rights you hold under mandatory local law.
