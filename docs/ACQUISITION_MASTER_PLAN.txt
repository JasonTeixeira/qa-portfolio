2. THE FUNNEL MODEL & STAGE KPIs
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ATTRACT  -> ENGAGE -> CAPTURE -> CLOSE -> RETAIN/REFER

  ATTRACT  (get found): organic search, content, social, referrals.
     KPIs: organic sessions, impressions, avg position, indexed pages,
           backlinks, branded search volume.
  ENGAGE   (earn attention): time on page, scroll depth, tool usage,
           pages/session, return visits.
     KPIs: tool completions, blog read-through, demo interactions.
  CAPTURE  (get the lead): magnets, forms, newsletter, booking.
     KPIs: lead magnet completion rate, email capture rate, booking rate.
  CLOSE    (win the deal): checkout, calls, proposals.
     KPIs: checkout conversion, call-booked rate, proposal win rate, revenue.
  RETAIN   (compound): retainers, referrals, reviews.
     KPIs: retainer MRR, referral count, repeat engagements.

TARGET FUNNEL MATH (set real baselines once GA4/PostHog are live):
  Traffic -> 2-4% to lead -> 20-30% lead-to-call -> 25-40% call-to-close.
  Track each step; optimize the weakest link, not the vanity metric.

 ADDENDUM v1.1 — THE PATH TO 99+ IN EVERY CATEGORY (AND COMPOSITE)

HONEST FRAMING (read first):
  A 99+ COMPOSITE is not earned by shipping tasks. It is earned by OUTCOMES —
  rankings, traffic, leads, closes — re-scored monthly against real KPIs. The
  programs below build a 99-CAPABLE platform; the score is then PROVEN by
  results. Anyone who promises a 99 from a build alone is selling theatre.
  Two things gate the whole thing and only YOU can supply them: real proof
  (named references) and live credentials. Without them the ceiling is ~85.

WHAT CHANGED FROM v1.0:
  v1.0 was a strong skeleton (~74). It would build content and HOPE. v1.1 adds
  demand validation, a near-term traffic bridge, a real link engine, a solo
  "lean path," money-page CRO, nurture, and baseline-first measurement — the
  delta from "competent" to "top-1%."
