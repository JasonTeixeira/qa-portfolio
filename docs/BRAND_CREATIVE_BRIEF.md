## 1. POSITIONING (the corrected angle)

**One line:**
> I build brands, businesses, and the AI systems that run them.

**Elevator:**
> Sage Ideas is a one-operator studio that builds *entire businesses* — the brand,
> the application, the SaaS, the high-converting website, the content & SEO, and the
> AI systems underneath. You hire one builder instead of stitching together five

**The frame that avoids "generalist":** breadth is sold as **integration and
end-to-end ownership**, never as a menu of "I'll do anything." One studio owns the
whole business. That's a *premium* story; "I do everything" is a freelancer story.

## 7. CREATIVE / ART DIRECTION (artistic, NOT AI slop)

**Anti-slop non-negotiables:**
- No generic SaaS template; no purple-on-white gradients; no stock hero with a
  centered headline + blob; no default fonts (Inter/Roboto/Arial); no cookie-cutter
  card grids.
- Distinctive type pairing with a real display face. Intentional, opinionated color.
- Real imagery / brand work as hero material. Pacing & contrast (not one texture
  repeated). One signature, memorable moment. Motion that's purposeful.
- **A brand-builder's site must prove TASTE and RANGE** — this is the portfolio.

**Three artistic territories to choose from (pick one, commit fully):**
1. **Editorial Atelier** — warm, magazine-grade, big expressive type, asymmetric
   layouts, the work as gallery. Reads "creative studio with serious taste."
2. **Gallery / Showcase-led** — the brands/products ARE the design; immersive,
   image-forward, museum-like framing, generous space. Best to show range.
3. **Refined Tech-Luxe** — dark or duotone, precise, but design-forward and warm
   enough to not read "just an engineer." (Evolution of the current direction.)

**Foundations:** a distinctive display + clean body pairing; one confident accent
palette (can flex per showcased brand); real photography (incl. a real photo of
Jason); compositor-friendly motion; WCAG AA; fast (premium = fast).
