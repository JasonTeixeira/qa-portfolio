3) CRITICAL ENGINEERING RULES (we hit ALL of these — DO NOT repeat)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- clamp()/calc() math MUST have SPACES around + and − operators
  (e.g. `clamp(3rem, 1.5rem + 6vw, 7rem)`). Without spaces the expression is INVALID
  and silently dropped -> tiny text + broken spacing. This bug burned hours.
- NO Lenis / smooth-scroll hijack — it caused bad lag and felt "annoying." Native scroll only.
- NO `content-visibility:auto` — it broke rendering / left blank sections.
- NO AI-generated decorative illustrations — they read as slop. Everything is code/SVG/canvas
  or REAL screenshots. (fal/Recraft vector icons were tried; the code-built UIs won.)
- Progressive enhancement: content VISIBLE by default; "animate-from"/hidden states arm ONLY
  after GSAP+ScrollTrigger confirm loaded (a `body.is-ready`/`is-reel` flag). reduced-motion
  OR lib-failure -> everything static + visible. A timeout catch-all reveals everything.
  Verify 0 CONTENT elements stuck at opacity:0 after a full scroll.
- Hero H1 must render LARGE (>90px @1440) and LEAD the composition (not bottom-jammed/tiny).
- prefers-reduced-motion: disable intro/pin/scrub/parallax/cursor; show everything static.
- Mobile 390px: NO horizontal overflow; disable the heavy pin/scrub; the work reel becomes a
  clean snap/tap full-screen-per-project sequence; everything visible/usable.
- Performance: GPU-only animations (transform/opacity/clip-path) + narrow will-change; PAUSE all
  loops/diagram/packet animations when offscreen (IntersectionObserver); throttle rAF; CLS ~0.
- 0 console errors (include `<link rel="icon" href="data:,">` to avoid the favicon 404).
- VERIFY THE FULL RENDER VISUALLY AT REAL SCALE (not thumbnails). Screenshot tip on this Mac:
  `npm exec -- playwright screenshot --full-page --viewport-size="1440,900" URL out.png`
  (run `npm exec -- playwright install chromium-headless-shell` once if it complains).
