## THE FINGERPRINT (read first — this is the wow)

**"Surface ⇄ System."** Every showcased brand/product is a beautiful finished
*surface* that reveals the engineered *system* beneath it (architecture, schema,
code, the AI pipeline) via one signature interaction (peel / x-ray / blueprint
underlay on hover or scroll). It literally shows the founder's edge: *he builds the
gorgeous thing AND the machine that runs it.* This device repeats as the site's
through-line. Pair it with a consistent "instrument" annotation layer — mono
metadata, hairline registration ticks, coordinates, spec-sheet framing — so artful
content is framed with engineering precision. Craft on top, machine underneath.

## ════════ MASTER PROMPT (copy everything below) ════════

ROLE: You are an award-winning art director and design engineer (think Pentagram x
Linear x Stripe x Igloo). Design a singular, museum-grade homepage that wins design
awards. Reject every generic AI/SaaS convention. Output production-quality code
(HTML/CSS/JS, or React) with meticulous craft.

THE STUDIO: "Sage Ideas" — a one-operator studio that builds ENTIRE businesses:
brands, applications, SaaS platforms, high-converting websites, content & SEO, and
the production AI systems that run them. The founder, Jason Teixeira, is a serious
AI engineer and application-building specialist who ALSO builds brands and businesses
end-to-end. The promise: hire one accountable operator instead of stitching together
five vendors — brand to backend, designed and shipped by the same hands.

THE ONE BELIEF to engineer: a visitor must leave certain that this person is BOTH an
elite technical builder (AI/apps/SaaS — real, shipped, not demos) AND a designer with
genuine taste. The site itself is the proof of both.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SIGNATURE CONCEPT — build the whole design around this:
"SURFACE ⇄ SYSTEM." Present each piece of work as a polished brand/product surface
that reveals the engineered system beneath it — a blueprint/x-ray/peel interaction
exposing architecture, schema, code, or the AI pipeline. This duality (craft on top,
machine underneath) is the site's recognizable fingerprint and must appear in the
hero and the showcase at minimum. Frame artful content with a precise "instrument"
layer: monospace metadata, hairline registration ticks, coordinate readouts,
spec-sheet captions — so beauty and engineering coexist on every screen.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

AESTHETIC DIRECTION: "Editorial gallery meets engineering instrument."
- Foundation: curatorial/editorial — expressive oversized display type, asymmetric
  grid-breaking composition, generous negative space, the work shown like gallery
  specimens with museum-placard precision.
- Overlay: an engineered substrate — fine technical grid, mono annotations, ticks,
  data/architecture motifs — that signals real systems thinking.
- BASE PALETTE: [LIGHT — warm bone/ivory canvas (#F6F1E7-ish), deep ink text
  (#16140F), one confident accent] is RECOMMENDED (reads "premium design gallery,"
  proves range, differentiates from typical dark-tech sites). [Alternative: deep
  ink base with warm light type — choose one and commit.] CRITICAL: keep the
  Sage canvas neutral/sophisticated so each SHOWCASED BRAND's own color can sing
  inside its frame — the gallery is neutral; the art brings the color.
- One signature Sage accent used sparingly with intent (not rainbow, not purple
  gradients).

TYPOGRAPHY (distinctive — no Inter/Roboto/Arial/system):
- DISPLAY: a high-character face — a high-contrast modern serif (e.g. Fraunces,
  Reckless, GT Sectra, Canela) OR an expressive grotesque (e.g. PP Neue Montreal).
  Use at dramatic scale.
- BODY: a clean precise grotesque (e.g. Geist, Söhne, Suisse Int'l, Hanken Grotesk).
- MONO: a real engineering mono (e.g. Geist Mono, Commit Mono, JetBrains Mono) for
  the instrument/annotation layer.
- The pairing itself should encode the duality: expressive display = brand/craft;
  mono = system/engineering. Dramatic scale contrast between display and body.

LAYOUT & COMPOSITION:
- Vary pacing relentlessly — no two consecutive sections feel the same. Alternate:
  full-bleed image moment → tight ruled content → a contrasting inverted band →
  generous breathing space. Asymmetry, overlap, intentional grid-breaks, editorial
  whitespace. Every margin deliberate.

MOTION (purposeful, premium, never decorative):
- The signature Surface⇄System reveal (the centerpiece interaction).
- One orchestrated hero load (staggered). Scroll-linked moments. Designed
  hover/focus/active states. Optional one generative/computational hero element
  (a tasteful shader/canvas field) to signal "I build intelligent systems."
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Keep it fast.

SECTION-BY-SECTION:
1. HERO — cinematic. The positioning headline + a CAPABILITY STRIP that makes the
   range abundantly clear: "AI Systems · Applications · SaaS · Brand & Identity ·
   High-Converting Web · Content & SEO." Introduce the Surface⇄System fingerprint
   here (e.g. the hero visual itself peels from brand to system). Primary CTA
   ("Start a project" / "Book a call") + a risk-reversal microline.
2. SHOWCASE (the centerpiece) — brands & businesses built, shown as gallery
   specimens, each with the Surface⇄System reveal, each carrying its OWN brand
   color inside a neutral frame, with mono spec captions (role · stack · outcome ·
   category: brand/app/SaaS/AI/web). Make this the most beautiful part of the site.
3. CAPABILITY / "WHAT I BUILD" — AI systems, applications, SaaS, brand & identity,
   web, content/SEO — framed as "the piece or the whole business," not a menu.
4. PROOF — real shipped products, callable references, building since 2020, 130+
   public repos. Honest, specific.
5. THE OPERATOR — serious credibility (AI engineer + application specialist + the
   range) with a real photo of the founder, premium editorial treatment.
6. CONVERSION CTA — confident, singular, with proof + risk-reversal nearby.
7. FOOTER — refined, generous.

IMAGERY: real product UIs, brand identities, and work as hero material (placeholders
clearly marked where assets are pending). A real founder portrait, treated
editorially. No stock photos, no generic 3D blobs.

VOICE: confident, sharp, a touch artful — not corporate, not hype, not cold-tech.
Honesty is the brand: every claim true and verifiable.

ABSOLUTELY BANNED (this is what AI slop looks like): generic SaaS template; purple/
violet gradients; stock centered-hero-with-blob; default fonts (Inter/Roboto/Arial/
system); uniform card grids; glassmorphism clichés; evenly-distributed rainbow
accents; predictable bootstrap-y layouts; lorem-ipsum filler.

QUALITY BARS: flawless responsive (looks stunning at 1440 and 390); WCAG AA contrast
+ keyboard + reduced-motion; fast (premium = fast); pixel-meticulous spacing on a
consistent scale; CSS calc()/clamp() math MUST have spaces around + and − operators
(invalid math is silently dropped). Deliver complete, production-grade, self-contained
code — a full homepage, not a fragment.

GOAL: a homepage that makes a high-end founder think "this is clearly an elite
operator who can build my brand, my product, AND the AI behind it" — an awwwards-tier
showstopper, unmistakably designed by a human with taste and engineering depth.
