SAGE IDEAS DISCORD CONTENT ENGINE BUILD PLAN
Version: 2026-06-21
Scope: Sage Ideas Discord, SageBot, Supabase, DeepSeek generation, RAG, content jobs, quizzes, challenges, leaderboard, onboarding, premium workflows.

PURPOSE
Build Sage Ideas Discord into a high-quality education community operating system where members always know what to do next, useful participation is rewarded, strong questions become content, and the server produces daily learning, interaction, proof, and reusable assets.

CORE STANDARD
The Discord should not feel like a passive chat room. It should feel like a live academy and build lab.

Every active day should produce:
1. A useful learning signal.
2. A specific question or discussion prompt.
3. A challenge or action members can complete.
4. A reason to answer or help someone.
5. A visible leaderboard or progress signal.
6. A content artifact candidate captured from real member activity.
7. A next step for new, active, premium, and inactive members.

NON-NEGOTIABLE QUALITY BAR
1. No generic AI slop.
2. Every bot post must have a clear member action.
3. Every generated post must pass quality checks before public posting.
4. Every role or point action must be backed by a database event.
5. Every scheduled job must be idempotent.
6. Every member-facing workflow must have a failure path and admin visibility.
7. No unapproved members should access member channels.
8. The server stays lean: no channel sprawl.
9. Content comes from real source material: member questions, shipped work, reviews, RAG docs, approved news inputs, and admin-approved themes.
10. Each phase must be tested and verified before moving to the next phase.

CURRENT FOUNDATION
Already built or partially built:
1. Lean 11-channel Discord structure.
2. Approval-gated onboarding with /apply, /approve, /reject, /pending.
3. Role assignment from application path and level.
4. Supabase tables for Discord members, applications, quizzes, challenges, points, messages, content queue, premium workflows, and gateway events.
5. SageBot slash commands for daily, quiz, challenge, leaderboard, ask-sage, content queue, premium review, office hours, and weekly recap.
6. RAG system over current site/docs corpus.
7. DeepSeek generation support for content generation.
8. Gateway worker foundation.
9. Approval audit/enforcement for existing members.
10. Discord smoke and unit test coverage.

KNOWN LIVE GAP
The server has not yet had real members pass through /apply and /approve after the reset. Until that happens, discord_members and discord_member_applications are expected to be empty.

TARGET MEMBER EXPERIENCE
New member:
1. Joins server.
2. Sees start-here only.
3. Accepts rules and reads the expectations.
4. Runs /apply.
5. Answers goal, experience, first build, path, level, support need, time budget, timezone, source, portfolio.
6. Waits for admin/mod approval.
7. On approval, gets Academy Member plus path and level roles.
8. Receives welcome and first-week checklist.
9. Completes first action within 24 hours.

Active member:
1. Opens daily-signal.
2. Completes quiz, question, or challenge.
3. Posts work in build-lab or questions.
4. Earns points for useful participation.
5. Sees weekly leaderboard and recap.
6. Gets nudged toward next useful action.

Premium member:
1. Gets Premium Member role through payment or admin grant.
2. Can submit premium reviews and premium questions.
3. Gets deeper critique and priority office-hours queue.
4. Has premium activity tracked separately for retention and value.

Admin/mod:
1. Reviews member applications.
2. Approves or rejects members.
3. Reviews AI-generated drafts.
4. Approves content queue items into public posts, resources, articles, lessons, or social assets.
5. Monitors leaderboard, unanswered questions, inactive members, content ideas, job health, and premium queue.

SERVER CHANNEL OPERATING MODEL
1. start-here
Purpose: rules, application instructions, quality bar, first action.
Permissions: unapproved members can view; cannot spam.
Bot behavior: explains /apply, approval process, and expectations.

2. daily-signal
Purpose: daily prompt, quiz, challenge, news-to-action update.
Permissions: approved members can discuss.
Bot behavior: posts scheduled daily content after approval or from trusted templates.

3. questions
Purpose: main Q&A and member help room.
Permissions: approved members.
Bot behavior: classifies questions, tracks unanswered items, awards helpful answers, captures strong questions into content queue.

4. build-lab
Purpose: project submissions, specs, progress, blockers.
Permissions: approved members.
Bot behavior: structures submissions, awards ship/progress points, promotes strong builds into wins and recaps.

5. review-queue
Purpose: code/design/AI/SEO/cloud/architecture critique.
Permissions: approved members; premium can get priority.
Bot behavior: tracks review requests, captures reusable lessons, routes premium reviews.

6. content-lab
Purpose: content ideas, captured questions, lessons, article/social/resource drafts.
Permissions: approved members, with admin workflow for publishing.
Bot behavior: queues items, labels source, supports approval buttons.

7. live-room
Purpose: office hours, live questions, notes, replay follow-up.
Permissions: approved members; premium priority.
Bot behavior: manages office-hours queue and recap.

8. resources
Purpose: approved templates, guides, prompts, useful tools.
Permissions: approved members; admin/bot posting preferred.
Bot behavior: posts approved resource drops and links back to source discussions.

9. wins-showcase
Purpose: shipped work, milestones, launches, proof, weekly winners.
Permissions: approved members.
Bot behavior: highlights wins, weekly recap, leaderboard.

10. premium
Purpose: premium critique, advanced drops, deeper answers.
Permissions: Premium Member only.
Bot behavior: premium review, premium ask, premium office hours.

11. team-ops
Purpose: private admin operations.
Permissions: Founder/Admin/Moderator only.
Bot behavior: application alerts, draft approvals, moderation reports, job errors, analytics summaries.
