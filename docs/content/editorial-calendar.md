# Editorial Calendar

Goal: make Sage Ideas findable for buyers and useful for builders without publishing generic AI content.

## Weekly Cadence

- Monday: publish one search-led article.
- Tuesday: convert the article into a LinkedIn post and X thread.
- Wednesday: publish one build note from Nexural, AlphaStream, Jobpoise, Trayd, or Sage Ideas itself.
- Thursday: send newsletter with one practical system, one proof link, one route into Studio or Academy.
- Friday: update internal links from older articles into the new post and one relevant service page.

## Quality Bar

- One claim per article must be backed by a real Sage project, public repo, screenshot, schema, or source.
- Every article gets one `OfferCTA`, one proof block, and at least one internal link to Studio, Academy, or a diagnostic tool.
- No fake metrics, fake client claims, or anonymous testimonials.
