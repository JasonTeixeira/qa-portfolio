# Newsletter And Nurture System

The newsletter should sound like an operator sharing systems from the floor, not a marketer reporting trends.

## Entry Points

- Blog article CTA
- Route Finder completion
- SEO audit report
- Academy track interest
- Contact form when the buyer is not ready for a sales call

## Welcome Sequence

### Email 1: The Surface And The System

Send immediately.

- Promise: Sage Ideas shows both the visible product and the operating system underneath.
- Proof: link to `/work/nexural` or the most relevant case study.
- CTA: run `/tools/route-finder`.

### Email 2: The Studio Route

Send after 2 days.

- Promise: if the buyer wants execution, the Studio route starts with diagnosis and scope.
- Proof: point to a service detail page and one case-study proof board.
- CTA: `/contact` or `/pricing`.

### Email 3: The Academy Route

Send after 5 days.

- Promise: if the reader wants to learn and build themselves, Academy gives them the operating model.
- Proof: link to `/academy`.
- CTA: choose a track or reply with the system they are building.

## Segments

- Studio buyer: high score, budget present, contact/route-finder source.
- Builder: academy click, newsletter source, course CTA.
- Researcher: blog-only, no project signal yet.
- Growth lead: SEO audit source, GSC/traffic language, content intent.

## Measurement

Track opens only as a weak signal. The meaningful events are replies, route-finder completions, booked calls, academy checkout starts, and qualified lead status changes in `/admin/leads`.
