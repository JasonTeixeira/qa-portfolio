# Repurposing Pipeline — Program H/B

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## One Asset Becomes Ten

Every flagship report, blog post, template, or case study should become:

1. Long-form page on sageideas.dev
2. LinkedIn post with one concrete lesson
3. X thread with 5-7 points
4. Newsletter blurb
5. Internal links from 3 related pages
6. Short “build note” for `/changelog`
7. One outreach opener for relevant prospects
8. One academy lesson idea
9. One template/checklist if the asset contains repeatable steps
10. One visual diagram or data card

## Publishing Order

1. Publish canonical page.
2. Add internal links.
3. Add schema and sitemap entry.
4. Share on LinkedIn/X with UTM tags.
5. Send newsletter blurb.
6. Add outreach angle to Acquisition OS.
7. Review GA4/GSC/PostHog after 7 days.

## Quality Rules

- No fake stats.
- No invented client quotes.
- No AI-generated decorative images as proof.
- Every claim links to a source, a repo artifact, a public page, or a clearly stated inference.
- Every content asset must route to a next step: audit, service, academy, template, or booking.

## Weekly Cadence

- Monday: choose one asset and update calendar
- Tuesday: outline and internal-link plan
- Wednesday: publish
- Thursday: distribute
- Friday: inspect early analytics and update the content backlog
