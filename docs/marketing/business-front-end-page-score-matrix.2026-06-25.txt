Scope: Public business, marketing, showcase, legal, SEO support, conversion-support pages.
Excluded from scoring: /admin, /portal authenticated app routes, /academy-admin, API routes, cron routes, internal admin showcase routes, and Academy curriculum pages unless they affect business positioning.

SCORING METHOD

Evidence levels:
- A = Directly audited in the 2026-06-25 business-site audit and screenshot run.
- B = Source inspected and included in sitemap/public route structure, but not in the 17-route audited set.
- C = Template-family score. The route is generated from a shared template; individual content varies and has not been manually reviewed one-by-one.
- D = Support/auth/utility route. Public route exists, but it is not a sales page.

Categories:
- Offer: how clearly the page says what Sage Ideas does or what the visitor can get.
- Copy: buyer-facing language, clarity, and lack of internal/operator wording.
- Design: visual polish, layout, spacing, and premium feel.
- Story: visual or narrative progression from problem to outcome.
- CTA: clear next action and path to demo/book/contact.
- Proof: proof, trust, evidence, specificity, and lack of unsupported claims.
- SEO: metadata, search intent, internal-link relevance, and indexability posture.
- Mobile/A11y: mobile readiness plus accessibility posture. Direct audit routes had 0 axe violations and 0 overflow.
- Perf: performance posture. Only Lighthouse-tested pages get fully earned high scores.
- Overall: brutal commercial posture from 1-100.
