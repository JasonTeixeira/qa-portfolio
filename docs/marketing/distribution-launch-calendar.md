# Distribution Launch Calendar

This is the Program H operating plan. It creates authority without pretending backlinks were earned before they exist.

## Thirty-Day Loop

| Day | Action | Asset | UTM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Publish flagship article | Blog + LinkedIn + X | `utm_campaign=flagship_article` |
| 2 | Submit updated sitemap in GSC/Bing | Sitemap | `organic` |
| 3 | Share case-study proof board | `/work/nexural` | `utm_campaign=proof_board` |
| 4 | Repurpose into short carousel | Visual proof board | `utm_campaign=proof_board` |
| 5 | Outreach to relevant newsletters/podcasts | Founder/operator angle | `utm_campaign=earned_media` |
| 8 | Publish academy build note | `/academy` | `utm_campaign=academy_route` |
| 10 | Add internal links from 10 older posts | Blog routes | `internal` |
| 12 | Publish service-specific guide | `/services/[slug]` | `utm_campaign=service_guide` |
| 15 | Ship a public diagnostic/tool update | `/tools/route-finder` | `utm_campaign=diagnostic_tool` |
| 18 | Publish case-study technical teardown | `/work/[slug]` | `utm_campaign=case_teardown` |
| 22 | Outreach to resource pages | Tool/report angle | `utm_campaign=backlink_outreach` |
| 26 | Newsletter recap | Route Finder + Academy | `utm_campaign=newsletter` |
| 30 | Review Search Console/PostHog | Program dashboard | `analysis` |

## Backlink Targets

- AI implementation directories
- SaaS founder newsletters
- Productized service roundups
- Technical SEO resource pages
- Startup tool directories
- Local founder/business communities
- Podcast guest pages

## UTM Rule

Every external share uses `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`. Use `docs/marketing/utm-conventions.md` as the source of truth.
