# Revenue-Backwards Model — Sage Ideas

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## Target

The working target is a content and conversion system that can support **$100k/month in revenue opportunity**, not guaranteed revenue. Actual revenue depends on close rate, capacity, offer mix, fulfillment bandwidth, and proof.

## Offer Mix

| Offer | Price | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sage Audit | $750 one-time | Low-friction diagnostic and trust builder |
| Build / AI / SaaS engagements | $9,500+ custom | Primary revenue |
| Care retainers | $600+/mo | Stabilization and recurring revenue |
| Academy | TBD | DIY revenue stream once packaging is approved |

## Funnel Math

Conservative target model:

| Metric | Assumption |
| --- | ---: |
| Monthly qualified visitors | 25,000 |
| Lead magnet / newsletter conversion | 1.5% |
| Qualified leads | 375 |
| Booked call rate from qualified leads | 6% |
| Booked calls | 22 |
| Close rate | 20% |
| New client engagements | 4 |
| Average first engagement | $12,500 |
| Monthly new service revenue | $50,000 |
| Retainer / follow-on expansion | $25,000-$50,000 |

Upside path to $100k/month requires either higher traffic, higher close rate, larger average engagement, or academy/productized revenue.

## Content Volume Required

Initial goal:

- 6 topic hubs
- 50+ improved existing posts
- 24 new high-intent posts
- 20 service × industry pages
- 1 flagship report per quarter
- 1 lead magnet that creates shareable proof

## Link / Authority Model

Target the first 90 days:

- 20 partner/vendor/profile links
- 10 audit badge links
- 5 podcast/newsletter/community mentions
- 5 guest or contributed posts
- 1 original report promoted across communities

## Constraints

This is a solo studio. The system should favor assets that compound:

- templates instead of one-off articles
- public reports instead of private-only audits
- programmatic pages with real differentiation
- repurposing SOPs instead of ad hoc posting
- measured funnels instead of vanity pageviews

## Review Cadence

- Weekly: leads, calls, content shipped, GSC query movement
- Monthly: traffic, conversion rate, top pages, lead quality, source attribution
- Quarterly: content pruning, new report, backlink audit, offer mix
