# SERP Briefs — Program G

Last updated: 2026-06-17

These briefs turn the seeded keyword map into buildable search intent. They are intentionally conservative until Search Console and third-party keyword data are connected.

## AI Development Studio

- Primary URL: `/`
- Primary query: `AI development studio`
- Intent: commercial investigation
- Buyer: founder, operator, or marketing/ops leader who wants AI/app/product work without hiring a full team
- 10x angle: not “AI agency” theater. Show the actual product surface and the system underneath it.
- Page requirements: clear solo studio positioning, real shipped products, AI/app/SaaS first, brand/growth as the wrapper, proof strip, work reel, booking CTA.
- Internal links: `/services/ai-development`, `/work`, `/tools/seo-audit`, `/academy`.

## AI App Development Services

- Primary URL: `/services/ai-development`
- Primary query: `AI app development services`
- Intent: commercial
- Buyer: company with a workflow they want automated or productized
- 10x angle: scope around evals, data quality, failure modes, and operations, not just “we use AI.”
- Page requirements: service schema, proof, timeline, what is not included, decision path, checkout/book routing.
- Internal links: `/lab/ai-readiness`, `/lab/calculators`, `/blog/the-ai-agent-boundary-problem`.

## Programmatic SEO Agency for B2B

- Primary URL: `/services/content-care`
- Primary query: `programmatic SEO agency for B2B`
- Intent: commercial
- Buyer: B2B SaaS/service company with thin content structure and weak conversion routing
- 10x angle: programmatic pages only ship when each page has unique data, useful comparison, and conversion path.
- Page requirements: content system examples, schema, internal linking model, crawl budget discipline, editorial SOP.
- Internal links: `/topics`, `/blog`, `/reports/ai-search-readiness-2026`.

## Fintech Software Development

- Primary URL: `/services/fintech`
- Primary query: `fintech software development`
- Intent: commercial
- Buyer: fintech founder/operator worried about payments, ledgers, KYC, audits, and shipping velocity
- 10x angle: speak in real engineering controls: idempotent webhooks, reconciliation, audit trails, RLS, evidence.
- Page requirements: no fake compliance claims, explicit “not your auditor/legal counsel” language, real fintech work links.
- Internal links: `/industries/fintech`, `/work/nexural`, `/work/alphastream`.

## Trading System Development

- Primary URL: `/services/trading-systems`
- Primary query: `trading system development`
- Intent: commercial
- Buyer: operator building market-data, backtesting, analytics, or execution-adjacent software
- 10x angle: emphasize evaluation, data hygiene, latency, explainability, and risk boundaries.
- Page requirements: no investment promises, architecture proof, AlphaStream/Nexural links.
- Internal links: `/work/alphastream`, `/lab/alphastream`, `/topics/fintech-trading`.

## QA Automation Consulting

## Cloud Infrastructure Consulting

- Primary URL: `/services/cloud-infrastructure`
